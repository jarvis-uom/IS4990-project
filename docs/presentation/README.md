## Who does what (slide 15)

Each of us owns one core research question from start to finish.

| Member | Area | Research question | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | AI water maps: labelled water masks of Sri Lankan tanks, a Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 deep-learning model, and a water-plant class for covered tanks | RQ1 | Water maps for all tanks |
| B | SWOT and gauges: SWOT heights from Hydrocron with poor readings removed, gauge data (daily records, history and field photos), and tests of whether "suspect" readings can be trusted | RQ2 | SWOT accuracy report |
| C | Storage and dashboard: a height–area–volume curve for each tank, storage every 5–12 days checked against official figures, the dashboard, and the forecast if time allows | RQ3, with RQ4 as a stretch | Storage series and dashboard |

All three of us: field visits on SWOT pass dates, labelling water masks, the thesis and the research paper.
